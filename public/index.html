Import CRM data

Paste the Ops Tracker (Sales Handoff) CSV export below, or choose the file. Each row becomes a contact plus a linked opportunity. Stage is derived from the Proposal and WIN / LOST columns (and from a status update that says the deal is dead), warmth is measured from Last Updated, and IEA Service Area, Opportunity Source and Segment are normalised. Rows are matched on client plus contact, so re-importing a refreshed export updates in place instead of duplicating.

Export contacts from the Field Portal as CSV, then paste the file below (or choose the file). Headers are matched loosely — Name, Company, Email, Phone, Title, Owner, Office, Division, Stage, Status, Last Contact, Site, Tags, Notes are all recognised, in any order. Rows are matched on portal ID, then email, so re-importing a refreshed export updates existing contacts instead of duplicating them.
